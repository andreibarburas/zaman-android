Zaman is a privacy-first calendar app built for Nextcloud. No ads, no tracking, no cloud lock-in, just your events, synced the way CalDAV was meant to work.

YOUR CALENDARS, YOUR SERVER
Connect directly to your Nextcloud instance over CalDAV. Zaman syncs your events in the background and keeps a local cache, so your agenda is always available, even offline.

BUILT FOR HOW YOU ACTUALLY PLAN
Today, Week, and Month views, each designed to stay readable instead of cramped. Color-coded calendars so you can tell your work, family, and personal events apart at a glance. A dedicated event detail screen with the date, time, calendar, location, and attendees laid out clearly. "Protect this time" lets you mark an event as a hard block, and Zaman will warn you before anything gets scheduled over it.

CONNECTED TO THE REST OF YOUR LIFE
Tap an attendee to see their name and photo pulled straight from your Vinci contacts, not just a bare email address. Tap a location to open navigation in whichever app you actually have installed, not just one specific provider. Calendar colors carry through into event details, so context is never more than a glance away.

MAKE IT YOURS
Light, dark, and OLED-friendly themes, adjustable text size, and a custom typeface pairing (DM Serif Display + Inter Tight) that gives the app its own character.

NO COMPROMISES ON PRIVACY
Zaman talks only to your Nextcloud server. Nothing is sent anywhere else, no accounts to create, no analytics running in the background. It's free, open-source, and built for people who want to own their own data.

FEATURES
- Direct CalDAV sync with your own Nextcloud, no DAVx5/ICSx bridge required
- Today, Week, and Month views
- Per-calendar color coding and toggles, including Nextcloud ICS subscriptions
- Event create, edit, and delete, with recurring events (RRULE)
- Event reminders, scheduled locally and restored after reboot
- "Protect this time" hard-block scheduling with conflict warnings
- Attendee linking to Vinci contacts, with name and photo
- Tap-to-navigate on event locations, using whichever app is installed
- Koto (Nextcloud Tasks) due-dates shown in the agenda
- Tavla (Nextcloud Deck) card due-dates shown in the agenda
- Biometric lock
- Light / Dark / OLED themes, adjustable text size
- Local-only mode with export/import for devices without Nextcloud
